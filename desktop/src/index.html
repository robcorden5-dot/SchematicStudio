Custom Symbols

PLC Module Creator

Makes a PLC module symbol: its details in a label block on the left, with the pins along the top and bottom edges, each labelled with its address and what it does. It goes into the library below, ready to drag onto a sheet.

Editing redraws this module. Modules already on a sheet are redrawn too; if you changed the number of inputs or outputs, check the wires on them.

Modules on a sheet are numbered from the designator: PLC1, PLC2…

Any input, output or supply can sit on either edge. Drag a row by its grip to reorder it, or to move it to the other edge.

Tick terminals with the boxes on the left, then group them (for example A, A_, B, B_, Z, Z_, V+, V- as X3 – Encoder). They are brought together on their edge and drawn in a frame with that title.

Drive Creator

Makes a drive symbol (variable speed drive, inverter or soft starter): a plain box with the make and model at the top, the supply along the top, the motor along the bottom and the control terminals in columns down the left and right sides (or, in the Classic layout, a label block on the left and the control terminals down the right edge). It goes into the library below, ready to drag onto a sheet.

Editing redraws this drive. Drives already on a sheet are redrawn too; if you changed the terminals, check the wires on them.

Drives on a sheet are numbered from the designator: VFD1, VFD2…

Tick terminals on one edge with the boxes on the left, then group them (for example A, A_, B, B_, Z, Z_, V+, V- as X3 – Encoder). They are drawn in a frame with that title.

Terminal Blocks

Find in project

Portfolio

Every sheet in the project. Tick a sheet to give it a tab, or untick it to keep the tab bar short on a big project — an unticked sheet is still in the project. Click a name to go to that sheet. Make sections (Power, Control, PLC I/O…) to organise the sheets as a tree, with sections inside sections. Drag a sheet onto a section to put it in there, or drag a section onto another to nest it. A section shows as a chip in the tab bar that collapses its tabs. Set a review state on each row, and use ⧉ to duplicate a sheet.

Interactive Symbol Creator

Tools:
100%

Snaps to the regular 10px grid below 1000% zoom; from 1000% up there is no snapping, so fine detail can be placed freely. Pins always snap to the regular grid, at any zoom. With no tool selected: drag on empty canvas to select multiple items (shift-click to add/remove one), hold Space and drag to pan instead. Ctrl+C/X/V to copy, cut, and paste the selection. Double-click text to edit it; press R (or the Rotate button) to rotate the selection 90°. Arc is click-based: click for center, click for radius, click for end angle.

Terminal Models

A terminal type, defined once: its part details and how many levels it has. A terminal that uses it follows any change made here, on every sheet, and the BOM takes its details from it.

Terminal Block Generator

A table with a column for each terminal block and a row for each position — and for each level of a double- or triple-level terminal — showing the wire ID(s) there, and the terminal model if there is one. It reads the terminals you have assigned to blocks, on every sheet.

Origin / destination needs a single block chosen above: the internal wire's is on the left of each row and the external wire's on the right. The table keeps these settings and updates itself as the wiring changes.
